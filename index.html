SQL

I learned SQL on the job to get this data

I came in without SQL. To answer the S-Mode question I learned enough to pull and check custom user cohorts from 700M Surface users myself, using query tools and templates, instead of waiting on someone else to pull them. The skill that mattered most was knowing what clean data should look like.

STEP 1
Build cohorts in SQLConsumer devices by activation month (Jul 2020 – Jun 2021), split into S-Mode and Windows Home.
STEP 2
Model lifetime valueOLAP pivots of trailing 1–5 year LTV for each revenue stream and cohort.
STEP 3
Check proportionalityCompared cohort counts against Monthly Active Devices.
STEP 4
RecommendCompared PSM at matching price points. My group finance manager took it to the S-Mode leads to inform how much more to invest in S-Mode.
Rebuilt from memory. These are not Microsoft files, data or screenshots. I recreated the method from memory to show how the work flowed: SQL to build the cohorts, Excel to model lifetime value, PowerPoint to make the case. Every figure is illustrative and the table names are made up.

Bringing Georgetown Law to Howard

We brought Georgetown Law’s Dean of Admissions, Dr. Andy Cornblatt, to Howard’s campus for a free info session. He was joined by the Hon. J.P. Howard III of the D.C. Court of Appeals and Howard alumni now at Georgetown Law. The first 30 senior attendees received application fee waivers.

Why I created the Micro-Scholarship

This was my idea. I kept seeing Howard students reach their last weeks on campus unable to pay the balance standing between them and finishing school. With the Howard University Alumni Association, we created the HUAA × HUSA Micro-Scholarship to close those gaps: $10,750 to 18 students.
